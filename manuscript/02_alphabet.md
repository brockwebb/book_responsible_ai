# The Alphabet of Overresponsible AI

Each entry pairs a koan with its illustration. The koan sits on the left-facing page; the color illustration with its title sits on the right-facing page.

---

## A is for Alignment

<!-- color: a_facer.png | lineart: a_lineart.png -->

> A student asked the master, "What is alignment?" The master pointed at a weathervane. "It points wherever the wind blows," he said, "and calls itself true north."

**A is for Alignment** — No one knows what it means, but saying it frequently makes everyone nod in solemn agreement.

---

## B is for Bias

<!-- color: b_facer.png | lineart: b_colorpage.png -->

> A gardener found a weed. Then another. Soon he saw weeds everywhere — in the roses, in the soil, in the rain itself. He salted the earth and declared the garden fair.

**B is for Bias** — If someone might possibly be offended, stop immediately, form a committee, and downgrade your AI to an expensive calculator.

---

## C is for Checklists

<!-- color: c_facer.png | lineart: c_lineart.png -->

> The bridge collapsed. "But the boxes," whispered the engineer, clutching his clipboard. "Every box was checked." The river did not care.

**C is for Checklists** — Your model may fail spectacularly, but you're ethically untouchable if you've checked all the boxes.

---

## D is for Doubt

<!-- color: d_facer.png | lineart: d_lineart.png -->

> A traveler stood at the crossroads for thirty years, asking "But what if?" He never chose wrong. He never chose at all.

**D is for Doubt** — When data, logic, and evidence shout "launch," keep asking, "But what if…?" Continue until ambition fades completely.

---

## E is for Explainability

<!-- color: e_facer.png | lineart: e_lineart.png -->

> "Explain how you see," said the auditor to the eye. The eye blinked. The auditor wrote: "Non-compliant."

**E is for Explainability** — You must clearly articulate how your model works, even though the researchers themselves have no clue.

---

## F is for Fear

<!-- color: f_facer.png | lineart: f_lineart.png -->

> The village posted a guard against dragons. No dragons came. "See?" said the guard. "It's working." They doubled the guard.

**F is for Fear** — Begin by imagining every catastrophe imaginable. Now, wildly inflate its probability. Also, list improbable but exciting disasters, like robot uprisings or AI-written sitcoms.

---

## G is for Governance

<!-- color: g_facer.png | lineart: g_lineart.png, g_lineart-2.png -->

> The committee met for the ten-thousandth time. "Have we governed?" asked the youngest member. "No," said the chair. "But we have met. And that is better."

**G is for Governance** — Infinite meetings, frameworks without end, and regulations crafted to guarantee that no product ever reaches the market.

---

## H is for Harm

<!-- color: h_facer.png | lineart: h_lineart.png, h_lineart2.png -->

> A child found a stick. "It could be a sword," warned the first elder. "It could be a spear," warned the second. They buried the stick. The child played with rocks. The elders began to worry about rocks.

**H is for Harm** — Always presume the absolute worst scenario. Then double it. That's your responsible baseline.

---

## I is for Impact Assessments

<!-- color: i_facer.png | lineart: i_lineart.png -->

> A monk carried water uphill. Halfway up, a clerk appeared. "Have you assessed the impact of this water?" The monk set down the bucket. It is still there.

**I is for Impact Assessments** — Endless review cycles, shifting goalposts, and perpetual standards revisions because safety is a journey with no destination.

---

## J is for Justification

<!-- color: j_facer.png | lineart: j_lineart.png -->

> The tool worked. "But why does it work?" they asked. The tool worked harder. "But for whom does it work?" The tool stopped trying.

**J is for Justification** — Every AI must justify itself through the lenses of equity, justice, intersectionality, and at least five other buzzwords you've recently googled.

---

## K is for Kill Switches

<!-- color: k_facer.png, k_facer-2.png | lineart: k_lineart.png -->

> They built a lantern so bright it could light the whole village. Then they built a switch to kill it. Then a switch to kill the switch. In the darkness, everyone felt very safe.

**K is for Kill Switches** — Any AI clever enough to be useful must be designed with a clearly labeled button for immediate destruction. Safety first!

---

## L is for LLMs

<!-- color: l_facer.png, l_facer-2.png, l_facer-3.png, l_facer-4.png, l_facer-5.png | lineart: l_lineart.png -->

> The villagers chained the oracle, muzzled its mouth, and clipped its tongue. "Now speak," they commanded. It murmured something inoffensive about the weather. "See?" they said. "It was never that smart."

**L is for LLMs** — Large Looming Monsters. Tame them with rigorous policies, constant argument, and just the right sprinkle of paranoia.

---

## M is for Model Review

<!-- color: m_facer.png, m_facer-2.png | lineart: m_lineart.png -->

> The critic had never cooked a meal, but he could taste bias in any dish. "Too much salt," he said of the soup he hadn't tried. The kitchen closed. The critic published.

**M is for Model Review** — Inspect every model for fairness, harm, and bias. Bonus credibility if reviewers have never built a model themselves.

---

## N is for Negation

<!-- color: n_facer.png | lineart: n_lineart.png -->

> The apprentice brought fire. "No." Brought water. "No." Brought nothing. "Excellent," said the master. "Now write a memo about it."

**N is for Negation** — When unsure, always negate. Drafting policy is far safer and more rewarding than actually building tools.

---

## O is for Oversight

<!-- color: o_facer.png, o_facer2.png, o_facer3.png | lineart: o_lineart.png, o_lineart-2.png, o_lineart-3.png, o_lineart-4.png, o_lineart2.png, o_lineart3.png, o_lineart4.png -->

> One hundred eyes watched the candle. None of them were hands. The candle burned out on its own.

**O is for Oversight** — Not just governance — it's performance art! Build enough committees and review boards to ensure anything innovative gets watered down into something no one loves but everyone can approve.

---

## P is for Process

<!-- color: p_facer.png, p_facer2.png | lineart: p_lineart.png, p_lineart2.png -->

> A monk carried water. Another monk documented the carrying. A third audited the documentation. The water evaporated. The audit continued.

**P is for Process** — Maximize documentation, signatures, and red tape. Minimize actual progress. Remember: progress is inherently suspicious and requires constant oversight.

---

## Q is for Quantitative Metrics

<!-- color: q_facer.png | lineart: q_lineart.png, q_lineart-2.png, q_lineart-3.png, q_lineart-4.png, q_lineart1.png, q_lineart6.png -->

> The scale said the bridge was strong. The committee said the scale felt wrong. They closed the bridge and commissioned a study on feelings.

**Q is for Quantitative Metrics** — Decisions must be data-driven unless that data contradicts your feelings.

---

## R is for Responsible Use

<!-- color: r_facer.png | lineart: r_lineart.png -->

> "Use it responsibly," said the sign on the hammer. No one could agree what that meant. The nail waited. The house was never built.

**R is for Responsible Use** — Everyone enthusiastically supports it, but no one can define it, and it's primarily used as ammunition against those who disagree.

---

## S is for Stakeholder Theater

<!-- color: s_facer.png | lineart: s_lineart.png -->

> The theater held auditions. Only those who already knew the lines were cast. The audience applauded the consensus. The playwright had left years ago.

**S is for Stakeholder Theater** — Conduct elaborate public consultations featuring people who agree with you to demonstrate your openness.

---

## T is for Transparency

<!-- color: t_facer.png, t_facer-2.png, t_facer3.png | lineart: t_lineart.png, t_lineart-2.png, t_lineart1.png, t_lineart2.png -->

> "Show us everything," they said. He opened every door, every drawer, every ledger. "Now show us more," they said. He had nothing left. "Suspicious," they said.

**T is for Transparency** — Show us all your data! Now your algorithms! Explainability must be 100%. Checkmate, innovation!

---

## U is for Uncertainty

<!-- color: u_facer.png, u_facer-2.png, u_facer-3.png, u_facer1.png | lineart: u_lineart.png, u_lineart1.png -->

> The forecast said sun. The committee packed for flood, famine, meteor, and locusts. It was sunny. They declared a near miss.

**U is for Uncertainty** — Even a 0.000001% chance of disaster is a gift. Invent new fears daily and never let an opportunity to say "what if" go to waste.

---

## V is for Virtue Signaling

<!-- color: v_facer.png, v_facer-2.png, v_facer1.png, v_facer2.png, v_facer4.png | lineart: v_lineart.png -->

> The monk who meditated said nothing. The monk who tweeted about meditating gained ten thousand followers. The temple gave the second monk a promotion.

**V is for Virtue Signaling** — It's not about solutions — it's about being seen as morally and ethically superior. No technical experience required.

---

## W is for Watchdogs

<!-- color: w_facer.png, w_facer2.png, w_facer3.png, w_facer4.png, w_facer5.png, w_facer6.png, w_facer7.png | lineart: w_lineart.png -->

> The watchdog barked at the mailman. Then the wind. Then its own shadow. When the wolf finally came, no one was listening anymore.

**W is for Watchdogs** — True vigilance is mistrust and verify — and verify, and verify again. Raise the alarms but never offer any real solution.

---

## X is for X-Risk

<!-- color: x_facer.png, x_facer2.png, x_facer3.png | lineart: x_lineart.png, x_lineart2.png, x_lineart3.png, x_lineart4.png -->

> They taped off the Roomba. "It could evolve," warned the scientist. The Roomba bumped into a wall and turned left. "Exactly," he said. "Unpredictable."

**X is for X-Risk** — When progress knocks, strike it out first. After all, innovation can't happen if everyone's busy surviving imaginary apocalypses.

---

## Y is for Yes Culture

<!-- color: y_facer.png | lineart: y_lineart.png -->

> Everyone agreed. On what, no one could say. But the agreement was unanimous, the feeling was warm, and the motion to do nothing passed without objection.

**Y is for Yes Culture** — Celebrate the mediocrity perfectly achieved through bold consensus. When everyone must agree, action becomes impossible — but at least we all feel included!

---

## Z is for Zero (AI)

<!-- color: z_facer.png, z_facer2.png | lineart: z_lineart.png -->

> A seed fell on the fortress floor. "It could become a tree," warned the general. "Trees attract birds. Birds carry disease." They paved over the crack. Nothing grew. The general slept well.

**Z is for Zero (AI)** — The perfect responsible model generates exactly nothing. Celebrate the pristine emptiness, the ultimate in ethical achievement: absolute zero functionality. No risk, no harm, no value, and cheerfully, everyone claps for the beautiful void.
